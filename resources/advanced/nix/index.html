    Warning

    I have just copied my flake onto this page, and have not gone through and cleaned it up. There may be things in there that do not work for you. It may be outdated and some of the things no longer necessary. You are on your own here.

    If you are using another Linux distro and do not wish to use Ubuntu, another alternative is to use nix-ros-overlay as a flake.

    This IS NOT RECOMMENDED for anyone who does not have a significant amount of experience using Linux, as you will likely run into issues that are much harder to debug than if you were just using Ubuntu.

    There is not a lot of documentation on doing this, so you will largely be on your own. You will break things, and will need to figure it out yourself.
